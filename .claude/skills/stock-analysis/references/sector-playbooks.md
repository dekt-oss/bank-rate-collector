# Sector Playbooks

## How to use this reference

Choose the playbook that best matches the principal earnings engine. Use only decision-relevant metrics. For diversified businesses, combine playbooks or use a sum-of-the-parts valuation.

## 1. Banks and diversified lenders

**Primary valuation**

- P/TBV and P/B versus sustainable ROE or ROTCE;
- residual-income or dividend-discount model when assumptions are supportable;
- P/E only after normalizing credit costs and one-offs.

**Operating KPIs**

- loan and deposit growth;
- NIM and funding mix;
- fee income;
- CET1 or equivalent capital ratio;
- NPL ratio, delinquencies, reserve coverage, net charge-offs;
- normalized credit cost;
- efficiency ratio;
- tangible book growth and share count.

**Key risks**

- asset-quality deterioration, deposit flight, duration mismatch, regulation, capital return restrictions, commercial-real-estate or sector concentration.

**Avoid**

- EV/EBITDA comparisons;
- treating high rates as uniformly positive without funding and credit effects.

## 2. Insurance

**Primary valuation**

- P/B or P/TBV versus sustainable ROE;
- embedded value, appraisal value, or dividend-discount approach where relevant;
- P/E using normalized underwriting and investment returns.

**Operating KPIs**

- combined ratio or loss ratio;
- premium growth and retention;
- new-business value, CSM, or equivalent local accounting metric;
- solvency and capital ratio;
- reserve adequacy;
- duration, reinvestment yield, and catastrophe exposure.

**Key risks**

- reserve underestimation, catastrophe losses, lapse behavior, asset-liability mismatch, regulatory capital changes.

## 3. REITs and property companies

**Primary valuation**

- P/FFO and P/AFFO;
- NAV premium or discount;
- dividend yield and implied cap rate;
- development value only with explicit completion and leasing assumptions.

**Operating KPIs**

- same-store NOI;
- occupancy and leasing spread;
- rent escalators and lease duration;
- AFFO payout;
- LTV, fixed/floating debt mix, maturity ladder, interest coverage;
- tenant and geographic concentration.

**Key risks**

- refinancing, cap-rate expansion, tenant failure, development overruns, external-management conflicts.

**Avoid**

- standard EPS or depreciation-heavy P/E as the primary metric.

## 4. Software, internet, and digital platforms

**Primary valuation**

- EV/revenue or EV/gross profit for early-stage or low-margin firms;
- EV/EBITDA, P/E, or FCF yield after margins become economically meaningful;
- DCF with explicit retention, margin, and stock-compensation assumptions.

**Operating KPIs**

- ARR or recurring revenue growth;
- net revenue retention, gross retention, churn;
- bookings, RPO, take rate, ARPU;
- gross margin and incremental margin;
- sales efficiency, CAC payback, Rule of 40;
- FCF conversion and stock-based compensation dilution.

**Key risks**

- decelerating cohorts, platform dependence, AI-driven disruption, customer concentration, aggressive capitalization, dilution.

**Avoid**

- ignoring stock-based compensation in “free cash flow” quality;
- comparing different revenue-recognition or marketplace models without adjustment.

## 5. Semiconductors and hardware

**Primary valuation**

- mid-cycle P/E and EV/EBITDA;
- FCF yield and replacement-cost or asset value where relevant;
- scenario valuation by unit volume, ASP, utilization, and margin.

**Operating KPIs**

- shipments, ASP, mix, backlog;
- utilization and inventory days;
- book-to-bill or equivalent demand signals;
- gross margin and operating leverage;
- capex intensity and node or product transition;
- customer concentration and supply constraints.

**Key risks**

- inventory correction, double ordering, export controls, technological transition, customer insourcing, excess capacity.

**Avoid**

- valuing peak-cycle earnings as permanent;
- treating all semiconductor subsectors as direct peers.

## 6. Autos, industrials, robotics, and capital goods

**Primary valuation**

- EV/EBITDA, P/E, FCF yield, and ROIC;
- sum-of-the-parts for captive finance, mobility, robotics, or listed affiliates;
- normalized cycle earnings rather than one peak year.

**Operating KPIs**

- unit volume, pricing, mix, incentives;
- order intake, backlog, book-to-bill;
- capacity, utilization, warranty costs;
- material and labor costs;
- capex, working capital, FCF conversion;
- installed base, service revenue, and recurring software or maintenance revenue.

**Catalyst validation for robotics or new mobility**

- technology readiness;
- commercial deployments and paying customers;
- production capacity and unit economics;
- ownership economics and minority interests;
- path from strategic option value to consolidated earnings and cash flow.

**Key risks**

- cycle downturn, tariffs, FX, labor, recalls, capex overruns, delayed commercialization.

## 7. Consumer, retail, and branded products

**Primary valuation**

- P/E, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield;
- DCF or unit-economics valuation for high-growth concepts;
- same-store-sales-adjusted peer comparison.

**Operating KPIs**

- volume, price, mix;
- same-store sales, traffic, ticket;
- gross margin, markdowns, inventory turns;
- store productivity and new-store returns;
- marketing efficiency, repeat rate, channel mix;
- working capital and lease-adjusted leverage.

**Key risks**

- demand elasticity, brand fatigue, inventory overhang, channel conflict, commodity input inflation, private-label pressure.

## 8. Healthcare services, pharmaceuticals, and biotechnology

### Profitable pharma and healthcare services

**Primary valuation**

- P/E, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield;
- risk-adjusted DCF for material pipeline assets;
- sum-of-the-parts when marketed products and pipeline have distinct economics.

**Operating KPIs**

- patient or procedure volume;
- price and reimbursement;
- product concentration and patent expiry;
- pipeline milestones and probability-adjusted value;
- R&D productivity, launch curve, gross-to-net deductions;
- cash conversion and legal liabilities.

### Pre-revenue biotechnology

**Primary valuation**

- net cash plus risk-adjusted NPV by program;
- probability-weighted milestone and commercialization scenarios;
- cash runway and expected dilution.

**Key risks**

- binary trial outcomes, regulatory delay, safety, manufacturing, reimbursement, financing and dilution.

**Avoid**

- headline P/E or EV/revenue where there is no commercial revenue;
- treating unadjusted TAM as asset value.

## 9. Energy, materials, mining, and commodity cyclicals

**Primary valuation**

- normalized or mid-cycle EV/EBITDA, P/NAV, FCF yield;
- asset-level NAV with commodity price, grade, recovery, capex, and discount-rate assumptions;
- balance-sheet resilience under a downside commodity case.

**Operating KPIs**

- production volume, realized price, cash cost, sustaining capex;
- reserves, mine life, decline rate, replacement ratio;
- project capex and commissioning schedule;
- hedge book and transportation differentials;
- net debt and liquidity.

**Key risks**

- spot-price reversal, cost inflation, permitting, country risk, project delay, reserve quality, environmental liabilities.

**Avoid**

- capitalizing peak spot prices indefinitely;
- comparing producers with materially different cost curves or asset lives.

## 10. Utilities, telecom, and regulated infrastructure

**Primary valuation**

- P/E, EV/EBITDA, dividend yield, FCF yield;
- regulated asset base or sum-of-the-parts;
- leverage- and rate-adjusted peer comparison.

**Operating KPIs**

- subscriber or customer growth, churn, ARPU;
- regulated return and rate-base growth;
- capex intensity and coverage expansion;
- leverage, interest coverage, debt maturity;
- dividend coverage and asset monetization.

**Key risks**

- regulation, spectrum or concession costs, refinancing, capex burden, competition, political price controls.

## 11. CDMO, ODM, and contract manufacturing

Applies to biologics/peptide CDMO, pharma CMO, cosmetics ODM, and any business whose revenue is booked against a customer's order rather than its own product demand.

**Primary valuation**

- forward P/E and EV/EBITDA against the order-backlog-driven revenue path;
- DCF built plant-by-plant (capacity x utilization x price), not top-down;
- revenue-per-unit-capacity versus peers to test pricing/mix quality, not just capacity size.

**Operating KPIs**

- order backlog and backlog-to-revenue coverage; new order intake by period (a backlog gap is a leading indicator years before revenue turns);
- plant-level capacity, utilization, and ramp-up schedule with commissioning dates;
- revenue per unit of capacity versus peers (a capacity leader with low revenue/capacity is a low-mix volume producer, not a premium franchise);
- customer concentration (share of top 1-2 customers) and big-pharma customer count;
- contract structure: take-or-pay/minimum purchase volume, currency of contract, pass-through of input costs;
- labor cost per revenue and wage-agreement status (labor is the main variable margin driver in high-fixed-cost plants);
- modality mix (antibody, ADC, mRNA, peptide, small molecule) and price per unit by modality;
- FX exposure share of revenue.

**Growth bridge requirement**

Build the forecast as: existing-plant base + each ramping plant's incremental revenue + new-modality/M&A contribution + other. Cross-check the company's implied revenue CAGR against the industry CAGR: growing in line with the market means share is being held, not gained, and a premium multiple then has to rest on margin or mix rather than growth.

**Key risks**

- new-order drought while backlog still supports current revenue;
- localization/tariff regimes shifting competition from unit cost to local capacity;
- wage/labor escalation in a high-fixed-cost base;
- margin dilution from acquiring lower-margin capacity;
- customer insourcing and single-customer loss.

**Avoid**

- treating capacity leadership as market leadership without revenue-per-capacity evidence;
- valuing an acquired business at the parent's multiple when its margin structure is materially lower.

## 12. Holding companies, brokers, and asset managers

**Holding companies**: value by sum-of-the-parts NAV with an explicit holding discount; track the discount against its own historical band and name the catalyst that would narrow it. Do not use consolidated P/E as the primary metric.

**Brokers and asset managers**: AUM growth and mix, brokerage market share, IB/trading revenue volatility, P/B against sustainable ROE. Do not apply bank playbook credit metrics.

## 13. Generic fallback

Use only when no sector playbook fits.

**Valuation**

- P/E, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield, P/B, or DCF according to profitability, capital intensity, and balance sheet.

**Core KPIs**

- organic revenue growth;
- margin and incremental margin;
- cash conversion;
- ROIC;
- leverage and dilution;
- customer concentration;
- reinvestment runway.

Explain why the selected methods fit the company and why excluded metrics do not.
